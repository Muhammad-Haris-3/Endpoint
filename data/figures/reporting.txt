   NCT05438901 is a single-group before/after study of hirudotherapy
   (leech therapy) recorded as ACTUAL enrolment of 12,317,546. That is
   not a plausible count for that design. It is over a fifth of the
   figure on its own, and it is almost certainly a data-entry error.

   Most other top contributors are behavioural megastudies - SMS nudges,
   online health advertisements, vaccine-booking reminders. They really
   did enrol millions, but "enrolled in a trial that never reported"
   invites a reader to picture something other than receiving a text.
